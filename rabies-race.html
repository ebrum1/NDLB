Rabies Race · a teaching game for The Epidemiology of Rabies Elimination

🐕 Rabies Race

No Dog Left Behind — reach the dogs that matter before the virus does.

One rabid dog is loose. Every infected dog hunts the nearest healthy dog, infects what it touches, and dies within 7 days — passing on a fresh 7-day death sentence each time. You are the vaccinator 💉. Touch a healthy dog to make it immune. Vaccination only works before infection — rabies has no cure. Immunity also wears off after about a minute, so coverage leaks and must be kept up.

You cannot do this alone. Free-roaming dogs are the fast spreaders that matter most, and they bolt the moment you approach — one person can't catch them in the open. Recruit volunteers 🧑‍⚕️ and your team will surround a roaming dog so it can't escape, then vaccinate it. Alone you'll only manage penned dogs, puppies, and dogs you pin against a wall.

You don't need every dog. Protect enough of the spreaders and transmission chains break: R falls below 1 and the outbreak burns out on its own. That is how rabies is really eliminated.

Susceptible — not yet protected Immune — vaccinated (collar) Infected — hunting, 7-day clock Dead — lost to rabies 🐕 free-roaming — bolts; needs a team to corner 🏠 penned — easy, but a dead-end for the virus 🐶 puppy — high value, fast, catch alone 🧑‍⚕️ volunteer — drive over to recruit

Controls: arrow keys / WASD, or move the mouse, or drag your finger. Your team follows your lead.

Difficulty

Tip: recruit early, then lead your team onto the roaming dogs and puppies. Leave the penned dogs for last.
