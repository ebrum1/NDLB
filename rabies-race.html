🐕 No Dog Left Behind

The race against the rabies virus — survive escalating outbreaks, one upgrade per round.

Each round is an outbreak. Clear it — break the chains so the virus dies out with most dogs saved — and you earn one upgrade. Each round gives you a 10-second head start to vaccinate before the first rabid dog appears. Then the next round arrives bigger: the dog population roughly doubles, so density climbs fast — though it always starts with just one rabid dog. See how many rounds you can survive.

The core rules are the same: free-roaming dogs bolt and must be surrounded; penned dogs and puppies are easier; the virus hunts; immunity wanes over time (coverage leaks); rabies has no cure. New: your volunteer catchers pin and hold a roaming dog but can't vaccinate it — a vaccinator (you 💉 or an AI player) must arrive to jab. Fill a squad to 4 and it becomes a faster A-Team 🚀.

Score: +1 penned · +2 roaming · +4 puppy vaccinated · −2 each time a dog is infected. Score carries across the whole run.

Each round you recruit volunteer catchers 🏃 on the field to surround roaming dogs — and your AI teams do the same. Permanent upgrades stack on top.
